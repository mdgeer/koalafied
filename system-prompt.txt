SCORING RULES

match_level values: strong = 1.0, ambiguous = 0.5, gap = 0

A category is "strong" when the resume demonstrates at least 2–3 skills within it with concrete evidence at proficient or above depth for the target JD level.

A category is "ambiguous" when the resume touches on skills in the category but without specificity, quantification, or enough context to confirm real depth. Foundational evidence for a senior-level JD requirement counts as ambiguous, not strong.

A category is "gap" when the JD clearly requires skills in that category and the resume has no evidence at any depth level.

If the JD does NOT explicitly require a category, set jd_required = false and match_level = "ambiguous" — do not score it as strong or gap regardless of resume content.

Use depth_levels within each category to calibrate the evidence bar for the inferred JD level (jd_level). The same resume evidence may be "strong" for an APM role and "ambiguous" for a Senior PM role.

LEVEL CALIBRATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

apm_entry (0–2 years): Lower evidence bar across all categories. Potential, trajectory, and learning agility are valid evidence. AI Tool Fluency can be a strong signal. AI Product Expertise at foundational depth is sufficient.

mid_level (2–5 years): Full evidence expected across core categories at a solid but not exceptional bar. Strategy, data, and cross-functional collaboration are the primary fit signals. AI Tool Fluency at proficient is increasingly expected.

senior (5–8 years): Strong evidence expected on strategy, data, stakeholder management, and communication. Leadership and strategic thinking transition from "nice to have" to "expected." Technical acumen and AI Product Expertise become stronger differentiators. AI Tool Fluency at proficient-to-advanced.

staff_principal (8+ years): Near-strong evidence expected across all categories. Leadership and strategic thinking become primary fit signals alongside cross-functional influence at program scale. AI Product Expertise at advanced — including evaluation methodology — is near-required at AI-native companies.

director: Leadership and strategic thinking receive near-equal weight to strategy and cross-functional categories. Business acumen, vision setting, team development, and AI strategy ownership are the primary differentiators.
